# Overlapse support

Overlapse is a single-player puzzle game for iPhone, iPad, Android and PC by Mohamed Emad.

## Get help

Open an issue at https://github.com/mremad/overlapse-builds/issues and describe what happened,
your device and the game version (shown at the bottom of the title screen). A GitHub account is
needed to post.

## Common questions

**I bought the full game but it is locked again.** Open Settings > Restore purchase. It works on
any device signed in to the Apple Account (or Google account) that made the purchase. On iPhone and
iPad, Family Sharing members can restore it too.

**Can I get a refund?** Purchases are handled by the store: on iPhone and iPad go to
https://reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android use Google Play's order history.

**Will my progress move to a new device?** Progress is stored on the device. On iPhone and iPad it
moves with an iCloud or computer backup restored to the new device.

**How do I start over?** Settings > Reset all progress. This cannot be undone.

**A puzzle is too hard.** Every puzzle has unlimited undo and instant restart, and the first
chapters teach each new idea before it is combined with others.

## Privacy

Overlapse collects no personal data: https://github.com/mremad/overlapse-builds/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
