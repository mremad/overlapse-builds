# Overlapse privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Overlapse is a single-player puzzle game by Mohamed Emad ("we"). This policy covers the Overlapse
app on Google Play (`com.mremad.overlapse`).

## What we collect

Nothing. Overlapse does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information.
It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, medals, Time Loop records and settings are saved only on your device, in the
app's private storage. Android may include them in your device backup (Google account backup) if
you have it turned on; that backup is managed by Google under your Google account's settings. You
can erase your progress at any time in Settings > Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

## Purchases

The optional one-time "Full game" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your
payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether this purchase belongs to the signed-in
Google account, so the game can unlock. Google's handling of that information is described in the
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

Overlapse is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
