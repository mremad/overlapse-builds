# Overlapse privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Overlapse is a single-player puzzle game by Mohamed Emad ("we"). This policy covers the Overlapse
app on Google Play and on the Apple App Store (`com.mremad.overlapse`).

## What we collect

Nothing. Overlapse does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information.
It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, medals, Time Loop records and settings are saved only on your device, in the
app's private storage. Your device may include them in its backup if you have backups turned on:
Google account backup on Android, iCloud or computer backup on iPhone and iPad. Those backups are
managed by Google or Apple under your account's settings. You can erase your progress at any time
in Settings > Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

## Purchases

The optional one-time "Full game" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the App Store.
We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether this purchase belongs to
your Google account or Apple Account, so the game can unlock. The stores' handling of that
information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
Apple Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

Overlapse is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mremad/overlapse-builds/issues, or use the developer
contact shown on the Overlapse page in Google Play or the App Store.
